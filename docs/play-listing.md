## Full description (max 4000)

```
Xpendiq turns your bank's transaction alerts into a clear picture of where your money goes — automatically, and entirely on your phone.

When your bank notifies you of a UPI payment, card spend, or credit, Xpendiq reads that alert, records the transaction, and sorts it into a category. No manual entry, no receipts, no spreadsheets.

★ Private by design
• Works 100% offline — there are no servers and no account to create.
• Your data never leaves your phone. Nothing is uploaded, shared, or sold.
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics.
• Source-available: inspect the code yourself at github.com/ka1z3n/xpendiq

★ Automatic tracking
• Reads bank transaction alerts from your messaging app (Google Messages, Samsung Messages) using notification access.
• Detects UPI, credit cards, debit cards, NEFT, auto-debits and more.
• Filters out OTPs and promotional messages — only real transactions are saved.
• Works with major Indian banks including HDFC, SBI, ICICI and Axis.

★ Understand your money
• Monthly spent, received, and invested totals at a glance.
• Spending broken down by category with a clear donut chart.
• Separate views for spends, credits, and investments.
• Foreign-currency (USD) spends folded into your totals using a manual rate you control.

★ You're in control
• Re-categorize anything — Xpendiq learns from your corrections.
• Create and manage your own categories.
• Optional app lock (fingerprint, face, or screen lock).
• Export your data to CSV any time.

Xpendiq needs Notification access to read your bank's transaction alerts. This is the app's core feature: it is used only to detect bank transaction messages, and everything is processed on your device. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.

This version uses no SMS permissions.
```
